01 / THE STUDY

About the Project

Understanding how connectivity shapes everyday life in rural communities.

⌁

Internet access. Village opportunities.

Internet connectivity has become an important part of education, communication, information and access to digital services. This Community Service Project studies how people in a rural community access and use the internet in their daily lives.

Through household visits and direct interaction with residents, the team aims to understand connectivity, usage patterns, awareness and the challenges faced by villagers.

⌖ FIELD STUDY AREA: NARASIMHA KONDA, DEVARAPALEM, ANDHRA PRADESH

The internet can help students find study materials, watch educational videos, attend online classes, practise skills and explore career information. It can support learning beyond the classroom, especially when reliable connectivity and suitable devices are available.

Key focus: learning resources · online classes · skill development · exam information

Messaging apps, voice calls and video calls make it easier for families to stay in touch with relatives, students and people working away from home. Online news and community groups can also help residents share useful information quickly.

Key focus: messaging · video calls · family connections · information sharing

Internet access can make essential services easier to discover and use, including digital payments, online banking, government information, health information and agricultural updates. Awareness and safe usage are important so residents can use these services confidently.

Key focus: UPI awareness · banking · e-governance · health and farming information

Rural internet use may be affected by network availability, signal strength, data affordability, device access, digital skills and online safety concerns. Understanding these barriers helps identify where awareness and better access can make a difference.

Key focus: connectivity · affordability · digital literacy · online safety

02 / OUR PURPOSE

Project Objectives

Key areas guiding our village-level study.

01

Understand access

Identify the availability of internet connections and devices among households.

Why this matters

Understand whether households have a reliable internet connection, what devices they use, and whether access is available to every family member.

⌁
02

Study usage

Understand how residents use the internet for education, communication, information and services.

What we explore

Learn about everyday uses such as online learning, messaging, video calls, news, information searches and access to digital services.

◎
03

Identify challenges

Listen to community members about connectivity, affordability, skills and awareness barriers.

What we listen for

Identify difficulties residents describe, including weak network signals, data costs, device availability, digital skills and awareness.

◇
04

Build awareness

Encourage informed and useful adoption of digital services and online resources.

What we encourage

Share practical awareness about useful online resources, responsible internet use and safer digital habits.

05 / PROJECT JOURNEY

Eight weeks of progress

Select a week to reveal its activities, focus areas and learning.

WEEK 01＋

Team formation and topic selection

The team discussed community needs, explored possible project themes and finalised “Internet Usage in Villages” with mentor guidance.

Focus areas

Team coordination · topic finalisation · role discussion

What this week contributes

The foundation week establishes the project purpose and the responsibilities needed for a coordinated community study.

WEEK 02＋

Village selection and planning

The field study area was selected and the team planned household visits, local interaction and the information to be gathered.

Focus areas

Village selection · visit planning · community interaction

What this week contributes

Planning helps the team approach residents respectfully and organise field activities.

WEEK 03＋

Survey design

Questions were prepared around internet access, devices, connectivity, daily usage, awareness and difficulties experienced by residents.

Focus areas

Question preparation · device access · usage patterns

What this week contributes

A clear questionnaire helps collect consistent responses from people with different ages and occupations.

WEEK 04＋

Data collection

The team visited households and interacted with residents to understand how they access and use the internet in everyday life.

Focus areas

Household visits · resident interviews · field observations

What this week contributes

Direct interaction provides practical insight into local usage patterns and challenges.

WEEK 05＋

Data entry and analysis

Survey responses were organised and reviewed to identify recurring themes in access, usage, awareness and connectivity challenges.

Focus areas

Response organisation · pattern review · observations

What this week contributes

Reviewing the collected information helps present findings in a clear and meaningful way.

WEEK 06＋

Awareness session

The project focused on discussing useful online services, responsible internet use and basic online safety with community members.

Focus areas

Useful services · safe browsing · awareness

What this week contributes

Awareness helps residents recognise useful online opportunities and take care while sharing information online.

WEEK 07＋

Documentation

Field photographs, notes and collected project records were arranged to support the project report and presentation.

Focus areas

Photographs · field notes · project records

What this week contributes

Good documentation keeps the project journey organised and makes the final report easier to prepare.

WEEK 08＋

Report, conclusion and presentation

The team brought together the project background, activities, observations and learning to prepare the final report and presentation.

Focus areas

Report preparation · conclusion · presentation

What this week contributes

The final week communicates what the team studied, what it learned and why rural digital access matters.
