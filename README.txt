HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open csp-main/index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Keep the assets folder, style.css and script.js together with index.html.
4. Internet is required to load the Google Fonts; the site itself works locally without a server.
